Gartner Predicts 70% of Enterprises Will Abandon Agentic AI Built by Vendor Forward-Deployed Engineering by 2028

  • By 2028, 70% of enterprises will abandon agentic AI built by vendor forward-deployed engineering (FDE) teams, trapped by soaring maintenance costs and unable to evolve the systems with their own staff.
  • Gartner warns of “FDE washing”: conventional consulting work marketed as specialized embedded engineering.
  • Through 2028, fewer than 20% of FDE engagements will turn recurring customer needs into capabilities in the vendor's core product, leaving buyers dependent on expensive third-party firms for changes.
  • Implication for buyers: contract for knowledge transfer and productized capability, not just a working demo.

Gartner Identifies Top Five Actions for CISOs To Take by End of 2026

  • Controlling AI agent access is one of the headline security gaps: 54% of organizations have no defined approach to limiting what AI agents can reach, or simply reuse the access model defined for humans.
  • Gartner's guidance for CISOs centers on treating agents as a distinct class of identity, with their own entitlements and lifecycle, before agent deployments scale further.
  • Framed as near-term actions to complete by the end of 2026, i.e. governance work that should run ahead of broader agentic rollouts rather than after an incident.
